04 / A FEW MORE DETAILS

Beyond the layout.

What have you explored in physical design?

Alongside accelerator implementation in TSMC 65 nm, I've developed a Python multi-bit flip-flop clustering optimizer and reusable Tcl flows in OpenROAD. In one CTS experiment, adjusting sink clustering reduced post-CTS max-fanout violations from 194 to 5 while maintaining timing closure.

What am I working toward?

I'm deepening my expertise in digital IC and physical design, particularly timing analysis, clock tree synthesis, and power, performance, and area optimization. I want to contribute to efficient compute hardware with an understanding of the complete path from architecture to silicon.
