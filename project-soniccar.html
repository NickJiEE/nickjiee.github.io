SonicCar

A voice-commanded autonomous vehicle with LiDAR navigation and deep learning stop sign detection.

As part of UCSD's ECE/MAE148 (Introduction to Autonomous Vehicles) course, our team developed SonicCar, a state-of-the-art autonomous vehicle capable of responding to voice commands and navigating intelligently and safely.

SonicCar integrates speech-to-text recognition with a large language model (LLM), enabling it to interpret and execute natural language commands. To ensure safe operation, it combines LiDAR-based obstacle detection with a camera-based stop sign recognition system trained using RoboFlow deep learning.

For more details, check out the GitHub repository!
